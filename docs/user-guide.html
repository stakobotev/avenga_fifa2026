1. Overview

Welcome to the FIFA World Cup 2026 Prode! This is an internal prediction game where you can compete with colleagues by predicting match results for the upcoming World Cup in USA, Mexico, and Canada.

The tournament features 48 teams divided into 12 groups of 4 teams each. You'll be able to predict the score for every match and earn points based on your accuracy.

Getting Started

After signing in, you'll land on the Dashboard where you can see your stats, upcoming matches, and make predictions right away.

2. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home base. It provides a quick overview of your performance and the most important upcoming matches.

What You'll See

  • Stats Cards: Your current rank, total points, exact score predictions, and total predictions made
  • Upcoming Matches: The next 6 matches with countdown timers
  • Bonus Predictions: Quick access to predict the World Cup Champion, Runner-up, Third Place, and Top Scorer

3. Making Match Predictions

Predicting match results is the core of the game. You can predict the final score for any match before it starts.

How to Make a Prediction

  1. Find the match - Go to the Dashboard or Matches page to find the match you want to predict.
  2. Enter your predicted score - Use the input fields to enter the home and away team scores.
  3. Click "Save Prediction" - Your prediction will be saved and locked when the match starts.
Important!

Predictions lock when the match kicks off. The countdown timer shows exactly how much time you have left. Make sure to submit before it reaches zero!

Knockout Stage Predictions

For knockout matches (Round of 32, Round of 16, Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, and Final), you'll also need to predict which team advances. This is important in case of a draw that goes to penalties.

4. Bonus Predictions

In addition to match predictions, you can make bonus predictions for extra points. These must be submitted before the tournament starts.

Available Bonus Predictions

Prediction Points Description
Champion +15 pts Predict the World Cup winner
Runner-up +10 pts Predict the losing finalist
Third Place +8 pts Predict the third-place winner
Top Scorer +10 pts Predict the tournament's top scorer
Deadline

Bonus predictions lock when the first match of the tournament kicks off. Make sure to submit all your bonus predictions before June 11, 2026!

How to Make Bonus Predictions

  • Team predictions: Simply click on a team's code (e.g., ARG, BRA) to select them. Your selection saves automatically.
  • Top Scorer: Type the player's name in the text field and click outside the box to save.

5. Leaderboard

The Leaderboard shows how you rank against other participants. It's updated in real-time as match results come in and predictions are scored.

Leaderboard Columns

  • Rank: Your current position among all participants
  • Player: Display name of the participant
  • Points: Total points earned from all predictions
  • Exact: Number of exact score predictions (worth 5 points each)
Your Position

Your row is always highlighted in purple so you can quickly find yourself on the leaderboard, even if you're not in the top positions.

6. Scoring System

Points are awarded based on the accuracy of your predictions. The more precise your prediction, the more points you earn!

Match Prediction Points

Result Points Example
Exact Score +5 points You predicted 2-1, result is 2-1
Correct Outcome +2 points You predicted 3-1, result is 2-0 (both home wins)
Wrong 0 points You predicted 2-1, result is 0-0

What Counts as "Correct Outcome"?

  • Home Win: You predicted the home team wins, and they did (regardless of exact score)
  • Away Win: You predicted the away team wins, and they did
  • Draw: You predicted a draw, and the match ended in a draw

Bonus Prediction Points

Prediction Points if Correct
World Cup Champion +15 points
Runner-up +10 points
Third Place +8 points
Top Scorer +10 points
Strategy Tip

Exact score predictions are worth more than double a correct outcome. If you're confident about a match, try to predict the exact score rather than just playing it safe!
